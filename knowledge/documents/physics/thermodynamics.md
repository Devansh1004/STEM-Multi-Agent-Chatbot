# Thermodynamics

Name: Thermodynamics

Definition:
The branch of physics dealing with heat, work, temperature, and energy transfer.

Laws:
- Zeroth law: If two systems are in thermal equilibrium with a third, they are in equilibrium with each other.
- First law: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred or converted.
- Second law: Entropy of an isolated system never decreases.
- Third law: Entropy approaches a constant value as temperature approaches absolute zero.

Properties:
- Heat flows from hot to cold regions.
- Entropy is a measure of disorder.

Common uses:
- Engines and refrigeration
- Chemical reactions
- Climate science
