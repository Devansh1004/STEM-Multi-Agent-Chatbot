# Newton's Laws of Motion

Name: Newton's Laws of Motion

Definition:
Three physical laws describing the relationship between a body and the forces acting upon it.

Laws:
- First law: A body remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a net external force.
- Second law: F = m*a, force equals mass times acceleration.
- Third law: For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Properties:
- The second law is the basis for computing force, mass, or acceleration when two are known.
- Units of force are newtons, mass in kilograms, acceleration in meters per second squared.

Common uses:
- Mechanics
- Engineering design
- Vehicle dynamics
- Projectile motion
