# Energy and Work

Name: Energy

Definition:
Energy is the capacity to do work, existing in forms such as kinetic, potential, thermal, and chemical energy.

Formulas:
- Kinetic energy: KE = 0.5*m*v^2
- Gravitational potential energy: PE = m*g*h
- Work: W = F*d*cos(theta)
- Power: P = W/t

Properties:
- Energy is conserved in a closed system.
- Total mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic and potential energy.

Common uses:
- Mechanical systems
- Thermodynamics
- Renewable energy analysis
