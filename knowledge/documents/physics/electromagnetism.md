# Electromagnetism

Name: Electromagnetism

Definition:
The branch of physics involving the study of the electromagnetic force, a combination of electric and magnetic forces.

Formulas:
- Coulomb's law: F = k*q1*q2/r^2
- Electric field: E = F/q
- Magnetic force on a moving charge: F = q*v*B

Properties:
- Electric charges produce electric fields, moving charges produce magnetic fields.
- Light is an electromagnetic wave.

Common uses:
- Electrical engineering
- Optics
- Motors and generators
