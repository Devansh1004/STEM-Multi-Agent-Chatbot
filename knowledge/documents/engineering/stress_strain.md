# Stress and Strain

Name: Stress and Strain

Definition:
Stress is the internal force per unit area within a material, strain is the resulting deformation.

Formulas:
- Stress: sigma = F/A
- Strain: epsilon = delta_L/L
- Young's modulus: E = sigma/epsilon

Properties:
- Stress is measured in pascals.
- Strain is dimensionless.

Common uses:
- Structural engineering
- Material science
- Mechanical design
