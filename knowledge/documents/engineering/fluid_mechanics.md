# Fluid Mechanics

Name: Fluid Mechanics

Definition:
The study of fluids and the forces acting on them.

Formulas:
- Bernoulli's equation: P + 0.5*rho*v^2 + rho*g*h = constant
- Continuity equation: A1*v1 = A2*v2

Properties:
- Applies to liquids and gases.
- Used to model pressure, flow rate, and velocity in pipes and channels.

Common uses:
- Hydraulics
- Aerodynamics
- HVAC systems
