# Golden Ratio

Name: Golden ratio

Symbol: φ

Approximate value: 1.618033988749895

Definition:
The golden ratio is the positive solution of the equation φ² = φ + 1.

Exact form:
φ = (1 + √5) / 2

Properties:
- The golden ratio is irrational.
- The golden ratio is algebraic.
- φ² = φ + 1.
- 1/φ = φ - 1.

Common uses:
- Geometry
- Fibonacci sequences
- Mathematical patterns
- Architecture
- Art and design
