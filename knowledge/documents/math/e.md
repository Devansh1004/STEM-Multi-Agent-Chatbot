# Euler's Number

Name: Euler's number

Symbol: e

Approximate value: 2.718281828459045

Definition:
Euler's number is the base of the natural logarithm.

Properties:
- e is irrational.
- e is transcendental.
- The natural logarithm ln(x) is the logarithm with base e.
- The derivative of e^x is e^x.
- The derivative of ln(x) is 1/x.
- The exponential function e^x is its own derivative.

Common uses:
- Exponential growth and decay
- Calculus
- Differential equations
- Probability
- Finance
- Physics
