# Pi

Name: Pi

Symbol: π

Approximate value: 3.141592653589793

Definition:
Pi is the mathematical constant defined as the ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter.

Properties:
- Pi is irrational.
- Pi is transcendental.
- The decimal expansion of pi is infinite and non-repeating.
- The circumference of a circle is C = 2πr.
- The area of a circle is A = πr².

Common uses:
- Geometry
- Trigonometry
- Calculus
- Fourier analysis
- Probability and statistics
- Physics and engineering
