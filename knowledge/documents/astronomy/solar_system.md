# The Solar System

Name: Solar System

Definition:
The gravitationally bound system of the sun and the objects orbiting it, including planets, moons, and asteroids.

Properties:
- Eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
- The sun contains about 99.8 percent of the system's mass.
- Planets orbit the sun in elliptical paths following Kepler's laws.

Common uses:
- Planetary science
- Space mission planning
