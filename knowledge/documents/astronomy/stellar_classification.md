# Stellar Classification

Name: Stellar Classification

Definition:
The categorization of stars based on their spectral characteristics and temperature.

Classes:
- O, B, A, F, G, K, M in order of decreasing temperature.
- The sun is a G-type main sequence star.

Properties:
- Temperature determines color, from blue (hottest) to red (coolest).
- Luminosity and mass correlate with spectral class on the main sequence.

Common uses:
- Star cataloging
- Astrophysics
