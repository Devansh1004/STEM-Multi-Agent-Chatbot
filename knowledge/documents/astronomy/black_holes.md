# Black Holes

Name: Black Hole

Definition:
A region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing, including light, can escape.

Properties:
- Bounded by the event horizon.
- Schwarzschild radius: r = 2*G*M/c^2
- Escape velocity at the event horizon equals the speed of light.
- Formed from the gravitational collapse of massive stars.

Common uses:
- General relativity studies
- Astrophysics research
