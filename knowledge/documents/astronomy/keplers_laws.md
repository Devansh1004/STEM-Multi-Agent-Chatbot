# Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion

Name: Kepler's Laws

Definition:
Three laws describing the motion of planets around the sun.

Laws:
- First law: Planets move in ellipses with the sun at one focus.
- Second law: A line joining a planet and the sun sweeps out equal areas during equal time intervals.
- Third law: The square of the orbital period is proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis.

Formula:
T^2 = (4*pi^2 / (G*M)) * a^3

Common uses:
- Orbital mechanics
- Satellite deployment
- Exoplanet detection
