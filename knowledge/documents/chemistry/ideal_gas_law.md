# Ideal Gas Law

Name: Ideal Gas Law

Definition:
An equation of state describing the behavior of an ideal gas.

Formula:
P*V = n*R*T

Properties:
- P is pressure in pascals, V is volume in cubic meters, n is moles, R is the gas constant 8.314 J/(mol*K), T is temperature in kelvin.

Common uses:
- Predicting gas behavior
- Chemical engineering calculations
