# Acids and Bases

Name: Acids and Bases

Definition:
Acids donate hydrogen ions, bases accept hydrogen ions, according to the Bronsted-Lowry theory.

Formula:
pH = -log10([H+])

Properties:
- pH scale ranges from 0 to 14.
- Values below 7 are acidic, above 7 are basic, 7 is neutral.

Common uses:
- Titration
- Biochemistry
- Environmental chemistry
